Gen AI Can Be Helpful But Can Be Harmful

I study both computer science and the humanities. At an advanced level in the humanities, data becomes essential to understanding complex topics. It's important to engage with both qualitative and quantitative data when conducting research. Sometimes, when I'm extremely busy with work and school, I may use AI to create graphs or visualizations. However, I think it's important to recognize the drawbacks of becoming too dependent on AI tools.

I'm a strong believer in finding the "fun" in fundamentals. That often means doing repetitive or tedious tasks, like manually converting data or writing basic code. Neuroscience suggests that such repetition helps our brains build stronger connections and deepen understanding. While I occasionally use AI to streamline certain processes, I believe it's crucial to first understand how those processes work—or to revisit them afterward—to truly grasp the concepts.

When using Gen AI to find an answer, whether that is for coding/tech or the humanities, it is important to not just use what you are seeing but to go back and learn it as well.
